How to read The Commons

This page replays the simulation. It does not change the rules or the numbers. Each villager is an agent from the recorded run.

  1. Huts and villagers. Every villager has a hut around the green. The roof color matches the villager. All villagers are the same size.
  2. Sacks are money. A villager's sack grows as they hold more coins and shrinks when they have less. The sack is the money indicator, not the body.
  3. The commons. The pond and bushes are the village's shared natural stock. It falls as the living cost consumes resources. The percentage in the header and on the pond is what is left.
  4. Exchanges. A gold coin is money changing hands. Gifts and repayments happen at the receiver's hut. Market trades happen in the market square, where coins and goods cross.
  5. Death. A villager at the resource floor is replaced by a tombstone and stops taking part.
  6. Explore. Press space to play or pause, use the arrows to step, drag the timeline to scrub, and click a villager or a feed row to inspect them.

Each iteration: gifts and repayments, then production and trade if the market is enabled, then consumption, then the living cost for every living villager.
